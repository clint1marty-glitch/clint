OSPF routing (open Shortest Path First)
- Multi Vendor Dynamic Routing
- implements Dijkstra Algorithm
- Administrative Distance of 110
- Advertised by Area (Area 0 is the  back bone)

OSPF States : DOWN > INIT > TWO-WAY > EX START > EXCHANGE


OSPF ROUTER ROLSE ELECTION: 

DS- Designated Router (master Router)
bdr- Back up Designated Roputer

recipe: 
router ospf # <Process ID>
router-id <ip, mostly loopback>
 netwrok (DCN) <wildcard DCN> Area #
